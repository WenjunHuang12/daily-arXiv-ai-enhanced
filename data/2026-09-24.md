### [1] [Small Cues, Big Consequences: Learning Pivotal Cues for Multimodal Meme Classification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26907)
*Akshit Sharma,Prashant W. Patil*

Main category: cs.MM

TL;DR: 该论文提出MemesCF基准（9,895条meme，覆盖harm/hate/sarcasm，标注关键证据的模态与理由）与MemePIVOT局部-全局分类架构，在HarMeme、PrideMM、MemeCF上一致优于强基线，证明显式关键证据建模可提升鲁棒性。

Motivation: Meme的冒犯、仇恨或讽刺含义常源于细微但决定性的视觉、文本或跨模态线索；现有依赖全局图文表示的多模态分类器会漏掉此类证据。

Method: 构建MemesCF数据集；提出MemePIVOT：使用冻结CLIP特征，以非平衡最优传输将词与图像块对齐（允许无关证据不匹配），并用证据融合头在不确定性下结合局部定位与全局meme语境。

Result: 在HarMeme、PrideMM与MemesCF上，较文本、图像、多模态及视觉-语言基线均取得一致增益；跨数据集与消融实验表明关键证据建模提升鲁棒性，并在全局多模态表示之外有实质贡献。

Conclusion: 显式建模关键证据是有效的meme分类方向；代码与数据集已公开（https://github.com/AkshitSharma1/MemePIVOT）。

Abstract: Memes often derive their harmful, hateful, or sarcastic meaning from small but decisive visual, textual, or cross-modal cues. Existing multimodal classifiers can miss such evidence when relying mainly on global image-text representations. We introduce MemeCF, a cue-focused benchmark of 9,895 memes across harm, hate, and sarcasm, with annotations identifying the modality and rationale of the pivotal evidence. We also propose MemePIVOT, a local-global architecture for meme classification. MemePIVOT uses frozen CLIP features, unbalanced optimal transport to align words with image patches while allowing irrelevant evidence to remain unmatched, and an evidential fusion head to combine local grounding with global meme context under uncertainty. Experiments on HarMeme, PrideMM, and MemeCF show consistent gains over strong text-only, image-only, multimodal, and vision-language baselines. Cross-dataset and ablation results further show that explicit pivotal-evidence modeling improves robustness and contributes meaningfully beyond global multimodal representations. Our code and dataset are publicly available at https://github.com/AkshitSharma1/MemePIVOT
