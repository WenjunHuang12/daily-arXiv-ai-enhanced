### [1] [TimelyRAG: Semantic-Temporal Hybrid Retrieval for Time-Critical Question Answering in Overlapping-Evolving Documents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11572)
*Youngeun Nam,Joeun Kim,Hwanjun Song,Susik Yoon,Jae-Gil Lee,Byung Suk Lee*

Main category: cs.IR

TL;DR: 提出TimelyRAG框架，将时间距离纳入排序以对齐查询与版本适配文档，并构建TimelyQABench基准，应对重叠演化环境下法规文档的检索增强问答难题。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLM与RAG在开放域问答中表现优异，但文档经修订演化后可靠性下降。现有时间敏感检索方法仅针对"非重叠演化"环境（每次更新为独立快照），而法律、政策、法规常处于"重叠演化"环境：修订覆盖旧条款却保留大部分内容，导致跨版本强语义重叠，现有方法失效。

Method: 提出TimelyRAG，一个与检索器无关（retriever-agnostic）的框架，将时间距离（temporal distance）引入排序过程，使查询与版本适配的文档对齐。同时构建TimelyQABench——首个面向法规密集型领域、聚焦重叠演化挑战的基准。

Result: 实验显示一致性能提升，nDCG@10最高提升+28.6%。

Conclusion: 时间推理对于在演化文档上实现可靠问答至关重要。所有资源开源于https://github.com/kaist-dmlab/TimelyRAG。

Abstract: Although large language models (LLMs) and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) have advanced open-domain question answering (QA), they remain unreliable when documents evolve through amendments. Existing time-sensitive retrieval methods address only the disjoint-evolving environment, where each update is an independent snapshot. However, laws, policies, and regulations often operate in overlapping-evolving environments, where amendments override earlier clauses while preserving most content, creating strong semantic overlap across versions. We propose TimelyRAG, a retriever-agnostic framework that incorporates temporal distance into ranking to align queries with version-appropriate documents. We also introduce TimelyQABench, the first benchmark for regulation-heavy domains with overlapping-evolving challenges. Experiments show consistent gains, up to +28.6% in nDCG@10, highlighting the importance of temporal reasoning for reliable QA over evolving documents. All resources are available at https://github.com/kaist-dmlab/TimelyRAG.

### [2] [Lower Bounds for Private Graph Optimization Problems using Reconstruction Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10877)
*Jacob Imola,Rasmus Pagh,Lukas Retschmeier*

Main category: cs.DS

TL;DR: 本文在差分隐私（DP）框架下研究图优化问题，基于重构方法给出了最小生成树、最小权完美匹配及层次聚类问题的新下界，改进了已有结果并揭示了近似DP在 $\ell_1$ 与 $\ell_\infty$ 邻接关系下的差异。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 已有工作（如 Sealfon, PODS'16）对DP图优化问题仅给出 $Ω(n/ε)$ 的较弱下界，且近似DP在 $\ell_1$ 邻接关系下是否能降低误差尚不明确；同时近似DP下基于图的层次聚类缺少参数化下界。本文旨在建立更紧、更具一般性的下界。

Method: 采用基于重构（reconstruction-based）的下界证明技术。考虑顶点集与边集公开、仅边权私有的图 $G=(V,E,\vec{w})$，在 $\ell_1$ 邻接关系下分别针对最小生成树与最小权完美匹配建立下界；进一步针对具有扩张性质的稀疏图族以及Dasgupta代价函数下的层次聚类问题推导参数化下界。

Result: 对最小生成树与最小权完美匹配，证明在 $n$ 个顶点、$m>2n$ 条边的最坏情况图上达到紧的 $Ω(n\log(m/n)/ε)$ 误差下界，且在 $δ\leq(n/m)^{Ω(1)}$ 时对近似 $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP 仍成立，改进了 Sealfon 的 $Ω(n/ε)$；对最小割至少为 $Ω(\log n)$ 的稀疏扩张图，给出最小生成树的 $Ω(n/ε)$ 下界；对Dasgupta代价的层次聚类，给出首个以平衡割最小权为参数的近似DP下界，推广了 Deng 等 (ICLR'25) 的结果。

Conclusion: 近似DP在 $\ell_1$ 邻接关系下不能降低MST的误差，与 $\ell_\infty$ 邻接关系下的结论形成鲜明对比；所提重构下界技术可推广至扩张图与层次聚类，为DP图优化问题提供了更紧、更一般的理论刻画。

Abstract: This paper studies fundamental graph optimization problems under differential privacy (DP) and shows new, reconstruction-based lower bounds. We consider a graph $G = (V, E, \vec{w})$ where the vertex set $V$ and edges $E$ are public and the weights $\mathbf{w}:E\rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ must be kept differentially private under an $\ell_1$ neighboring relation.
  For the problems of releasing a minimum-weight spanning tree and a minimum-weight perfect matching, we show new, tight error bounds of $Ω(n\cdot\log(m/n)/ε)$ on worst-case graphs with $n$ vertices and $m>2n$ edges. The upper bounds are known pure DP algorithms while the new lower bound holds even under approximate $(\varepsilon,δ)$-DP as long as $δ\leq (n/m)^{Ω(1)}$. Our lower bounds improve the $Ω(n/ε)$ lower bounds of Sealfon (PODS~'16). The fact that approximate DP does not reduce error for MST under the $\ell_1$ neighboring relation contrasts with the recent upper bound of Pagh et al. (PODS~'25) which shows that approximate DP allows much better error under the $\ell_\infty$ neighboring relation.
  Going beyond worst-case graphs, we give lower bounds for large families of sparse graphs with expansion properties. We show a lower bound of $Ω(n / ε)$ for the minimum spanning tree for any graph where the minimum cut is at least $Ω(\log(n))$. Finally, we consider the problem of private hierarchical clustering under Dasgupta's cost function (STOC~'16) and show the first approximate DP lower bound parameterized by the minimum weight of a balanced cut. This extends lower bounds of Deng et al. (ICLR~'25) to general graphs and to approximate DP.

### [3] [The Privacy Subsidy in Market Microstructure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10543)
*Yuki Nakamura*

Main category: cs.GT

TL;DR: 隐私保护交易所基于粗化订单流定价；本文证明承诺对粗信号做后验均值定价的做市商必然向交易者转移"隐私补贴"，并给出闭式刻画与福利评估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 隐私保护型交易设计只观察到订单流的粗化信息，却需对已结算的精细订单流清算。核心问题是：能否在仅依赖粗信号的前提下同时实现信息有效定价与零利润？若不能，其福利代价为何？

Method: 对一般粗化建立不可能性定理；在三个经典微观结构模型（单期Kyle高斯噪声、Glosten-Milgrom二元方向通道、连续时间Kyle-Back布朗通道）中闭式求解补贴；将补贴与LVR（Loss-Versus-Rebalancing）做结构对应，分解为噪声驱动平方项与承诺对象因子；引入盈亏平衡手续费并分析其造成的交易量扭曲与死重损失，在显式配置价值下做阶数比较；最后内生化隐私水平以确定最优噪声尺度。

Result: 无法在粗信号上同时实现有效与零利润：必然存在闭式隐私补贴。补贴的福利率与LVR同构，均为噪声驱动平方乘承诺对象因子。总补贴为纯转移，可由盈亏平衡费收回；但该费用扭曲交易量，产生的死重在噪声尺度上是四阶，而总补贴为二阶，故隐私在一阶上福利中性且不可逆损失更小。内生化隐私水平后，在差分隐私收益与死重之间权衡可得闭式内点噪声尺度，且保持价格冲击与知情强度的"半揭示"乘积不变，同时使价格冲击的波动率弹性偏离教科书值1。

Conclusion: 隐私保护交易设计存在根本性权衡：粗化定价必然产生向交易者的补贴转移，但其不可逆福利损失仅为高阶小量，隐私在一阶意义上福利中性；最优隐私水平为内点解，并在不破坏核心微观结构关系的前提下重定价冲击弹性。

Abstract: Privacy-preserving exchange designs price on a coarsened view of order flow. We show that a market maker committed to informationally efficient (posterior-mean) pricing on a signal strictly coarser than the flow it settles necessarily cedes a closed-form welfare transfer to traders -- the privacy subsidy -- and that no rule restricted to the coarse signal is simultaneously efficient and zero-profit against the settled flow. We establish this impossibility for a general coarsening, then characterise the subsidy in closed form across three canonical microstructure models: single-period Kyle with Gaussian flow noise, Glosten-Milgrom with a binary direction channel, and continuous-time Kyle-Back with a Brownian channel. The subsidy obeys a structural correspondence with Loss-Versus-Rebalancing, both welfare rates factorising as a squared noise driver times a committed-object factor. Gross of fees the subsidy is a pure transfer recovered by a break-even fee; once levied, that fee distorts volume, and the resulting deadweight -- under an explicit allocative value of trade -- is fourth-order in the noise scale while the gross subsidy is second-order, so privacy is welfare-neutral to leading order with a strictly smaller irrecoverable loss. Endogenising the privacy level, a protocol trading a differential-privacy benefit against this deadweight chooses an interior noise scale in closed form; doing so leaves the half-revealing product of price impact and informed intensity intact while unpinning the volatility-elasticity of price impact from its textbook value of one.

### [4] [ABRA: An algorithm which cannot converge to low-quality Nash equilibria](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11889)
*Vartika Singh,Philip N. Brown*

Main category: cs.GT

TL;DR: 提出近似最优响应算法（ABRA），通过噪声与理性参数，使多智能体次模协调博弈跳出不稳定劣质均衡，收敛时系统目标严格高于最优值的50%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 次模目标的多智能体协调问题中，对应博弈的纳什均衡仅保证在最优解的50%以内，且近期工作表明达到该最坏界的最差均衡并不稳定。作者希望利用这一不稳定性，设计算法跳出劣质均衡，从而获得优于50%的系统目标。

Method: 设计近似最优响应算法（ABRA），由噪声参数和理性参数控制。噪声用于逃离劣质均衡，理性参数用于平衡噪声带来的目标函数损失。针对任意两人博弈分析其收敛行为：若收敛到纳什均衡，则系统目标严格大于50%最优值加一个由噪声参数控制的项；否则收敛到某常返类，类中若含低于50%最优的动作剖面，必同时含有最优剖面，或含有高于50%最优且超出量相同并附加噪声控制因子的动作剖面；在低效动作剖面中的停留时间可由理性参数控制。

Result: 理论上证明ABRA收敛到纳什均衡时系统目标严格超过最优值的50%并附加噪声控制项；在常返类情形下，低效动作剖面与最优或更优动作剖面共现，且停留时间可控。数值仿真显示，最小期望目标函数通常远高于最优值的一半。

Conclusion: ABRA能突破次模协调博弈中50%的经典最坏界保证，通过噪声与理性参数的调节有效逃离不稳定劣质均衡，理论分析与仿真均验证了系统目标的改善。

Abstract: We consider a game theoretic approach to solve multi-agent coordination problems with submodular objectives. It is known for such problems that the Nash equilibria for the corresponding game are always within 50% of the optimal. A recent work further shows that the equilibria which achieve this worst-case bound are not stable. Leveraging this, we design an Approximate Best Response Algorithm (ABRA) governed by a noise parameter and a rationality parameter. The noise allows ABRA to escape the bad equilibria and the rationality parameter balances any degradation in the objective function caused by the noise. We show for any two-player game that if ABRA converges to a Nash equilibrium, its system objective value is strictly more than 50% of optimal plus a term controlled by the noise parameter. Otherwise, ABRA converges to some recurrent class: if a recurrent class contains any action profile yielding system objective less than 50% of the optimal, the class must also contain either the optimal action profile or an action profile yielding system objective strictly more than 50\% of the optimal by the same amount in addition to a factor controlled by noise parameter. The time that ABRA spends in such action profiles can be controlled using the rationality parameter. Using numerical simulations, we show that the minimum expected objective function is typically well above half of the optimal.

### [5] [Strategic Information Transmission over Gossip Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10576)
*Emirhan Tekez,Melih Bastopcu,Sinan Gezici*

Main category: cs.GT

TL;DR: 研究全连接gossip网络中节点追踪二元连续时间马尔可夫源的Stackelberg博弈，用随机混合系统(SHS)框架分析发送者与接收者的交互。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 发送者在通信预算限制下策略性发送更新，接收者通过gossip交换包并决定是否跟随发送者，需要刻画这一交互的均衡性质。

Method: 将交互建模为Stackelberg博弈，采用随机混合系统(SHS)框架进行分析，并辅以蒙特卡洛仿真验证。

Result: 证明发送者预算约束在每个内点最优处都紧致，将问题简化为一维预算线搜索；当发送者以更高速率推送其偏好状态时，接收者以最高可用速率gossip；gossip不具方向性，与发送者策略的不对称性对抗而非强化；证明乐观Stackelberg均衡存在，且在gossip上限下策略半边可行时唯一且可显式刻画。

Conclusion: 在gossip网络中，sender-receiver交互的乐观Stackelberg均衡在特定条件下唯一且可显式表达，仿真与分析递推一致。

Abstract: We consider a fully connected gossip network of $n$ nodes that track a binary continuous-time Markov source through a strategic sender transmitting updates under a communication budget at a rate that depends on the source state. The receivers exchange packets through gossip and decide whether to follow the sender. We model this interaction as a Stackelberg game and analyze it through a stochastic hybrid systems (SHS) framework. We prove that the sender's budget constraint binds at every interior optimum, reducing its problem to a one-dimensional search on the budget line. When the sender pushes its preferred state at the higher rate, the receivers gossip at the highest available rate. Gossip has no direction of its own and works against the asymmetry in the sender's policy rather than reinforcing it. We prove that an optimistic Stackelberg equilibrium exists, and that it is unique and explicitly characterized whenever a policy on the strategic half of that line is feasible at the gossip cap. Monte Carlo simulations agree with the analytical recursion.

</details>
