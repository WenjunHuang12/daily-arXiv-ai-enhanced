<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 将问题等价转化为在均匀超图中高效寻找短环，进而表述为算法化的 Moore 界（Moore bounds）；通过归约到高斯消元型算法（folklore 算法 I）并推广 [AF09] 的技术进行分析。

Result: 归纳并对比四类算法区域：I. β>1, δ>1 的高斯消元法；II. β≥k/2 的生日悖论法；III. β>δ+k(1−δ), δ∈(0,1) 的归约算法 [DJ24, CSZ24]；IV. 严格改进为 β>δ+(k−1)(1−δ), δ∈(0,1)。

Conclusion: 给出了带噪 k-XOR 测试可高效求解的样本-噪声参数相图，并将最优区域从 δ+k(1−δ) 严格收紧到 δ+(k−1)(1−δ)，其本质是超图中短环查找与算法化 Moore 界问题。

Abstract: This mostly expository note studies the sample-noise regimes in which the testing problem for noisy $k$-XOR in dimension $n$ can be efficiently solved. The current state-of-the-art algorithms are: I. A folklore algorithm when $β> 1, δ>1$ based on Gaussian elimination; II. A folklore algorithm when $β\ge k/2$ based on the birthday paradox; III. Extending [AF09], we strictly improve the last regime to $β> δ+ (k-1)(1-δ), δ\in (0,1).$ These algorithms correspond to efficiently finding short cycles in uniform hypergraphs and can be equivalently phrased as algorithmic Moore bounds.
