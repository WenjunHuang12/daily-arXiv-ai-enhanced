### [1] [Asymptotically Optimal Public Project Redistribution without Bounded Precision: A Complex-Analytic Proof](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10995)
*Mingyu Guo*

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 已有工作表明最差情况效率比随n增长趋近于1,但依赖一个强有界的精度假设(所有类型为分母有界的理数),该假设实际上把困难情形限制为除有界个代理人外几乎都报告0且报0者精确知道总量;去掉假设后已知最佳保证退化为1/2。本文旨在移除该假设。

Conclusion: 在任意实数类型下,以复分析工具可得确定性、匿名、策略证明且无赤字的VCG再分配机制,其最差情况效率比接近1,显著优于无假设情形下的1/2保证。

Without it, the best known guarantee tends to 1/2. We remove the assumption. After m rounds the aggregate error is at most 1/Hm mean reports, where Hm is the mth harmonic number. The proof of this bound is the heart of the paper and, unusually for a result about payments, uses complex analysis: a trigonometric representation of the shortfall function together with the Poisson-Jensen inequality for bounded analytic functions in the upper half plane.

Abstract: We establish a previously unnoticed connection between commitment (BC), a fundamental cryptographic primitive, and deterministic identification (DI), a post-Shannon communication setting. Specifically, for additive white Gaussian noise channels $\mathcal{G}$ we show that a reliable commitment protocol can be obtained from deterministic identification codes in both the honest but curious and fully dishonest security settings. This viewpoint allows BC schemes to inherit the asymptotic performance of DI codes. Indeed, we show that commitment is naturally achievable in the linearithmic regime, i.e., with message sets of size $N_n=\exp [Θ(n\log n)]$, and we establish a lower bound on the linearithmic-scale BC capacity of $\dot C_{\text{hBC}}(\mathcal G)\geq\frac12$ in the honest but curious case and $\dot C_{\text{BC}}(\mathcal G)\geq\frac14$ in the fully dishonest picture. This refines the best previously known characterisation of the commitment capacity over $\mathcal{G}$, which established only an infinite linear-scale capacity.
